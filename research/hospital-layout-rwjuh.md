# RWJUH New Brunswick: what the layout is built from

Written 2026-10-10 by the layout workstream. Companion to `catalog/hospitals/rwjuh_nb.layout.json`
(generated by `catalog/hospitals/build_layout.py`).

## Summary

- **No interior floor plan of the hospital or its emergency department was found.** The layout's
  interior is a reconstruction, not a survey. No room is tagged `surveyed`.
- What is real in the file: the exterior footprints and street centrelines (OpenStreetMap, tagged
  `surveyed`), where the emergency department sits in the complex and which street its entrances
  face (hospital map and construction notices), and the zones and counts the hospital has stated
  (tagged `published`).
- What is ours: the shape of the department, the split of "more than 100" treatment spaces into
  pods, every room position, and the whole inpatient block (drawn at ground level beside the
  department so the flow is visible in one view; in reality inpatient units are on upper floors).

## OpenStreetMap use and attribution

Exterior footprints and roads in `site.buildings` and `site.roads` tagged `surveyed`, and the file
`catalog/hospitals/rwjuh_nb.osm_site.json`, are derived from OpenStreetMap data,
© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License 1.0
(https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The app should show "© OpenStreetMap contributors"
wherever the site outline is drawn.

Coordinate frame: equirectangular metres about 40.49543 N, -74.44992 E (centroid of way 251382262;
the coordinates given in the task brief, 40.4955, -74.4497, are 20 m from it), then rotated 34
degrees so rooms can be axis-aligned with the hospital's building grid. **In this layout +x points
34 degrees north of east (along Somerset Street towards Easton Avenue) and +z points 34 degrees
east of south (towards French Street).** Site origin is local (-100, -85); site is 240 m x 170 m.

Taken from OSM: the main hospital outline (drawn as a 0.3 m ground slab so the modelled rooms sit
inside it), the Bristol-Myers Squibb Children's Hospital, the Cancer Institute of New Jersey, the
Jack and Sheryl Morris Cancer Center, two hospital buildings that carry no name in OSM, and
Somerset Street, Little Albany Street, French Street, Division Street and Hardenbergh Street.
Building heights are **not** in OSM for most of these; they are assumed (the JSON says which).
OSM carries no indoor data and no emergency-entrance node for this hospital.

## How the reconstruction was placed

- The department block (55 m x 104 m, 5,726 m2, about 61,600 sq ft against the published 60,000)
  sits in the north-east of the real footprint with its public face to the east, towards the
  north-south leg of Little Albany Street. That matches the visitor map and the notices.
- Walk-in entrance at the north end of that face (nearest the Somerset / Little Albany
  intersection, where the hospital says emergency drop-off is). Ambulance entrance and the
  eight-space bay further south on the same face, next to resuscitation and decontamination.
  The real ambulance court is the notch in the footprint at the bend of Little Albany Street; the
  modelled bay is within about 20 m of it. Exact door positions are inferred.
- Inside: waiting, three triage rooms, care initiation and results waiting behind the walk-in
  entrance; fast track (10) and the pediatric department (17) beyond them; four adult pods
  (18, 18, 16, 16 = 68 rooms) around nurse stations with the imaging band (2 CT, 2 X-ray,
  2 ultrasound), pharmacy and lab between the pod rows; six resuscitation bays on the street side.
  68 + 6 + 10 + 17 = 101 treatment spaces, consistent with "more than 100".
- Inpatient block west of the department: five 24-bed units (the 120-bed pool the simulator admits
  to), a 24-bed ICU (published by the builder), step-down, operating rooms, recovery and one MRI
  at low detail. Capacities other than the ICU are assumed.

## What could not be found or opened

- **Any architectural floor plan** of the department or hospital: none found.
- Healthcare Design magazine photo tour
  (https://healthcaredesignmagazine.com/projects/photo-tour-robert-wood-johnson-university-hospital-emergency-department/):
  HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, and a Cloudflare bot check in both headless and headed Chromium
  (Playwright). Not opened; nothing from it is used. Search-result snippets attribute to it:
  Francis Cauffman as fit-out architect, Array Architects for core and shell, a nine-phase
  project, and a 12-room behavioral health unit. These are **unverified** and not in the layout.
- Becker's Hospital Review, Daily Targum, New Brunswick Today (planning board report), MyCentralJersey:
  all refused the fetch. Not used.
- The older lettered campus map on cinj.org
  (https://www.cinj.org/sites/cinj/files/documents/RWJUHCampusMap.pdf): the URL now returns the
  site's web page, not a PDF.
- Internet Archive: offline / rate-limited at the time.
- New Brunswick planning board filings: no primary documents located.
- Architects' own project pages (Francis Cauffman / FCA, Array Architects): none located.
- The partial interior "Rapid Admit" map PDF listed in the teammate's research was not re-fetched
  (it concerns admitting and surgery waiting, not the ED).
- Overpass API was overloaded; the main OpenStreetMap API was used instead.
- Not published anywhere we could read: number of triage rooms, adult bays by zone, fast-track
  spaces, CT / X-ray / ultrasound counts, total resuscitation bays, waiting-room size, which floor
  the department is on, bed counts by inpatient unit.

## For a human to verify

1. Walk-in and ambulance door positions against a current aerial photo or a site visit.
2. The pediatric count (17) against the hospital or the local-press articles.
3. Counts tagged "assumed" in `catalog/hospitals/rwjuh_nb.yaml` (triage 3, fast track 10,
   resuscitation 6, CT 2, X-ray 2, ultrasound 2).
4. Building heights in `rwjuh_nb.osm_site.json` (assumed).
5. If a real plan is obtained: replace rooms in the JSON (or add a tracing step to the generator)
   and tag them `surveyed`; nothing else needs to change.

## Rebuild

```
.venv/bin/python catalog/hospitals/build_layout.py            # writes the three layout files
.venv/bin/python catalog/hospitals/build_layout.py --check    # validate only
.venv/bin/python catalog/hospitals/build_layout.py --refresh-osm data/raw/layout/osm/map_bbox.osm
```

The build validates against `contracts/layout.schema.json`, checks room types against
`contracts/enums.yaml`, unique ids, site bounds and that no two rooms overlap. Downloads and
previews are in `data/raw/layout/` (gitignored).
