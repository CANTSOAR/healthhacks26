## Current synthesis

The recommended product is a faculty-controlled clinical training environment: educators create reusable patients, students practice encounters beside a progressive chart, and feedback links to actual questions/actions and objectives. This is the working foundation for the broader virtual-hospital vision.

Challenge 1's key reusability proof is a new faculty-created patient that runs immediately without patient-specific code changes. A prepared case or a recorded demo alone does not establish that path. Use shared case versions across dialogue, chart and assessment; faculty controls disclosure and approval. Preserve explicit unknowns and evidence for disputed feedback.

The user's current preparation sequence starts with research organization, ontology and a populated teaching knowledge base. Database design and AI scenario generation follow. The [ontology plan](ontology-and-knowledge-base-plan.md) translates this into entities, source acquisition, relationships, constraints, owners and readiness gates.

The first content should come from the supplied nutrition/communication profiles, selected and corrected with a mentor. An ER visual setting is optional; it does not convert those profiles into acute-care cases. A new acute chest-pain case needs independently authored and reviewed facts/artifacts. Staffing, queues, multi-professional journeys, detailed geometry and predictive physiology retain separate future acceptance gates.
