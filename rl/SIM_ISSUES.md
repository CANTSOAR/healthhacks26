# Simulator issues met by the RL workstream (nothing in `sim/` or `catalog/` was edited)

Observed against simulator fingerprint `b4fd91bc587860a4` (01:50, milestone 1) unless a later one is named.

1. **No "patient-minutes waited this hour" metric.** `metrics.yaml` reward term `wait` says all not-yet-seen
   patients accrue per hour; `door_to_provider_sum_min` is only booked when a patient is first seen (and never
   for LWBS). Workaround in `rl/env.py::reward_terms`: `waiting_count * 60` (end-of-hour census of not-yet-seen
   patients). Request: a step metric `waiting_patient_minutes` integrated over the 12 ticks.
2. **`arrivals` excludes diverted ambulances**, so the hourly `arrivals` series differs between two managers on
   one seed when one of them diverts. The exogenous series is `arrivals + diverted`; the common-random-number
   test (`rl/tests/test_managers.py::test_rollouts_validate_and_share_patients`) asserts on that sum.
3. **One clock per batch, one scenario per simulator.** `sim._tick` is a scalar, so hospitals cannot reset
   independently and one `HospitalSim` cannot mix scenarios. Workaround: synchronous resets, and
   `rl.env.ConcatEnv` steps one simulator per scenario (costs one extra per-tick latency each).
4. **GPU throughput is latency-bound.** Measured on the RTX A5500 with `python -m sim.bench --device cuda`
   (default actions, step + observe): B=256: 31 hospital-days/s (28 ms/tick); B=1024: 126 (28 ms/tick);
   B=4096: 267 (53 ms/tick); B=16384: 288 (198 ms/tick). So about 6,900 environment steps per second is the
   ceiling for everything sharing the GPU. CPU on deepstack (2 threads): B=1: 13 ms/tick; B=32: 21 ms/tick;
   B=512: 99 ms/tick. A tick is many small tensor ops; fewer, larger ops (or torch.compile) would raise the ceiling.
5. **The fixed-default baseline is not stationary over 30 days** (normal_week, seeds 100000-100063, 30 days):
   mean wait 188 min, p95 wait 909 min, LWBS 15.5%, boarding 736 patient-hours/day, while the first 2-3 days sit
   near 45 min mean wait and 4% LWBS. The ward pool fills and boarding blocks bays. Reward scales and the
   constrained objective's threshold (0.75 x baseline p95 = 675 min) inherit this; both are regenerated by
   `rl/scripts/measure_scales.py` when the calibration changes.
6. **Cost is almost insensitive to staffing.** At baseline `cost_labor` is about 3.0k of 33.7k USD per hour;
   `cost_fixed` (16.3k) and `cost_variable` (14.3k, proportional to patients treated) dominate. Across very
   different managers `cost_per_day` only moves between roughly 0.78M and 0.83M, and the cheapest way to cut cost
   is to treat fewer patients. A `min_cost` manager therefore has little honest room.
7. **Python-side logs only for B <= 8** (`event_log_max_batch`): `snapshot()["events"]`/`["decisions"]` are empty
   for larger batches, so rollouts are recorded one hospital per process on CPU. The recorder builds its own
   decision list (with readable labels) by diffing `sim.settings`.
8. **`invalid_actions` counts call-in requests above the per-shift cap** (a rule rejection, not a programming
   error); the rule-based managers trigger it when a queue stays above the call-in threshold. Harmless.

Status 02:20: the orchestrator reports the simulator agent is addressing 1, 4 and 5. `rl/env.py` uses
`waiting_patient_minutes` automatically once `sim.step()` returns it. The whole RL pipeline is regenerated
(scales, tuning, training, evaluation, rollouts) after `sim/M2_READY`.
