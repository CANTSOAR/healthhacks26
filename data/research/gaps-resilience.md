# EHR downtime and model validation

Four located guidance extracts are in [the structured records](gaps-resilience.json). The [SAFER guide](https://healthit.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/SAFER-Guide-2.-Contingency-Planning-Final.pdf), practices 2.1–2.3, supports independent communications, recovery reconciliation and staff preparation. These are recommendations, not RWJ performance measurements.

[AHRQ modeling guidance](https://digital.ahrq.gov/health-it-tools-and-resources/evaluation-resources/workflow-assessment-health-it-toolkit/all-workflow-tools/simulation-modeling) supplies the process for gathering and validating local model inputs.

**Inference:** model EHR recovery and outstanding documentation as separate states. No outage rate, recovery-time distribution or training-effect size has been assigned. Local downtime drills and approved event traces would replace these unknowns.
