# RWJUH New Brunswick public service and procedure inventory

This inventory adds 17 short, source-attributed facts from official RWJBarnabas Health public pages. It records publicly described services, procedures, and published equipment or bed counts. A published count or service listing is evidence of reported facility capability; it does not establish live availability, staffed capacity, current operating status, or a slot for a particular patient.

## Evidence and scope

- The adult hospital and named services are Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital (RWJUH), 1 Robert Wood Johnson Place, New Brunswick. The pediatric critical-care records refer specifically to The Bristol-Myers Squibb Children's Hospital (BMSCH) at RWJUH; outpatient imaging refers to University Radiology at Robert Wood Johnson on Plum Street; proton therapy refers to the Laurie Proton Therapy Center. These settings remain explicit and are not interchangeable.
- RWJUH's trauma page reports Level I adult trauma status and nearly 2,500 trauma patients annually, without specifying the volume's measurement year or method. A separate ED page uses a larger combined ED-and-trauma denominator; it is not substituted here.
- Cardiac Services reports seven adult cath labs including two EP labs. Another RWJUH program page says over 12,000 procedures across eight laboratories. The count discrepancy is retained as a limitation; this inventory does not reconcile it. The reported annual procedure volume is not live workload.
- Pediatric trauma designation wording is inconsistent across RWJBH pages: the BMSCH PICU page says nationally accredited pediatric trauma center, the RWJUH ED page states Level II verification, and a system pediatric trauma page says Level I. The record preserves capability wording without choosing a level.
- Service menus are not clinical protocols. Proton treatment selection and transplant candidacy require case-specific clinical assessment. No model infers treatment eligibility, timing, or availability from these records.
