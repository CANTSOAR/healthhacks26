## Evidence boundaries

The records use only four distinct sources: ACR, NICE, ACEP, and NHS. Their recommendations come from different settings and have different authority: ACR addresses imaging appropriateness, NICE provides UK fracture-service recommendations, ACEP supplies professional ethics guidance, and NHS gives patient-facing urgent-care advice. They support conditional actions and workflow resources, not the likelihood that a branch occurs, staff completion times, local capacity, or a measured return rate. No probability, medication dose, or local performance value is assigned. Treat every `inferred_scenario_use` as authored scenario design, separate from `source_evidence_basis`.

The ACEP item is currently search-index-only and pending primary inspection; it is not acquired evidence and should be excluded from evidence-backed model behavior until the original is inspected. The NHS page was inspected directly and distinguishes injury-time NHS 111 symptoms from immediate A&E/999 triggers and recovery/cast concerns routed to NHS 111. NICE hot reporting is recommendation 1.1.9 (confirmed against its recommendation page's indexed section). Numeric intervals are source-stated: ACR's 10–14 days is the repeat-radiograph option, and no other interval is inferred here. Existing BOAST and NSW follow-up recommendations remain preserved in `encounter-treatment.json`; differences between external service targets should remain attributed to their sources rather than reconciled into a purported RWJ schedule.
