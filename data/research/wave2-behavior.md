# Qualitative simulation inputs

Added15 limited source-grounded descriptions across two AHRQ instruments:10 safety-culture composite topics and 5 teamwork-observation topics. They provide concrete axes for writing and reviewing hospital interactions: workload, speaking up across hierarchy, error handling, handoff completeness, team coordination, and shared awareness. They are definitions, not collected observations of local staff.

Source PDF/page locators, original factual paraphrases, and source item counts are preserved per record. We did not redistribute full instruments or individual survey questions. These derived descriptions are not an official survey and inherit no psychometric validation. No AHRQ or HHS endorsement is implied.

Sources: [AHRQ Hospital Survey2.0 composite document](https://www.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/sops/surveys/hospital/hospitalsurvey2-items.pdf), [TeamSTEPPS observation tool](https://www.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/wysiwyg/teamstepps-program/tools/ts-team-performance-tool.pdf), [AHRQ reuse FAQ](https://www.ahrq.gov/faqs/index.html?page=3), [SOPS naming/adaptation guidance](https://www.ahrq.gov/sops/about/using-sops.html).

For our simulation, a domain reviewer can use these topics to define observable interaction goals and failure modes. It would be incorrect to infer how often local employees display each behavior or to claim a validated learner rubric solely from these topic summaries. We still need reviewed scenario dialogue, patient perspectives and context-specific scoring criteria.
